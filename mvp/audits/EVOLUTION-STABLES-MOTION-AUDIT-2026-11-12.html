Primary: Jakub Krehel — Production Polish

The production landing page has a coherent, restrained premium motion language: capped scroll reveals, slow line growth, hover light sweeps, accordion expansion, editorial carousels and a masked glass nav. Most effects support hierarchy or continuity. The main trust gap is shared chrome: the mobile menu and account dialog still snap out of existence. The main accessibility gap is continuous showcase motion that keeps changing after the visitor asks for less motion.

The mobile navigation is conditionally mounted with no enter or exit transition, so it snaps open and closed. The account dialog fades in over 800ms, then vanishes immediately on Escape or close. Both are primary navigation surfaces and should share a state-driven enter/exit language.
